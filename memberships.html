How It Works

Banking, not bargaining

AVEN MED memberships are not discount programs. They are not coupon clubs. They are a quieter, more deliberate way to care for yourself over time.

Each month, your contribution accumulates as treatment credit on your account. Use it when you're ready — on the treatments that fit your plan, at your pace. Large investments become smaller decisions, made gradually, made well.

Credits apply toward treatments and services. Not retail or merchandise.

The Difference

Why we don't discount

Most aesthetic practices reward you for spending more. We reward you for staying — and for caring for yourself consistently, the way good care actually works.

Discounts make sense for one-time purchases. They make less sense for the kind of care that compounds across years. Our memberships are built for the second kind.

"We measure success not by how much you've saved, but by how steadily you've invested in yourself."

Good to Know

Membership questions

How do membership credits work?
Your monthly contribution is added to your AVEN account each month. When you're ready for a treatment, the credit is applied toward it. Unused credits carry over month to month. They're yours.
What can I use credits toward?
Credits apply to all in-practice treatments and services. They do not apply to retail skincare, merchandise, or third-party products.
Can I pause or cancel?
Memberships can be paused or cancelled. Any accumulated credit remains on your account and can be applied to treatments at any time.
Is there a contract?
AVEN Essential is month-to-month with no commitment. AVEN Elevate is an annual relationship — designed for patients ready to invest in continuity.
How is my monthly contribution determined?
In consultation. We build a plan based on your goals, your timeline, and the kind of care that fits your life. The number isn't pre-set — it's considered.
Is this a discount program?
No. AVEN MED memberships are not discount memberships, coupon clubs, or loyalty programs. They are a way to invest in your care over time — steadily, deliberately, well.
