# AVEN MED — Education Content Roadmap & Local SEO Topic Map (Phase 8.7)

**Status:** Planning only. No articles written, no pages created, no site/runtime changes.
**Purpose:** A prioritized, machine-referenceable editorial program to make AVEN one of the
strongest *local organic* resources in its market — attracting the *right* patients through
education-first, medically responsible, brand-restrained content anchored to the Orland Park
location.

**Guardrails honored throughout:** approved services only (no Hormone Optimization / BHRT /
Peptide Therapy — Phase 8.2); single approved identity **Alaa Mashal, MSN, APRN, FNP-BC —
Founder & Clinical Director**; "physician-directed" framing for weight loss (collaborating
physician); no invented credentials, years, awards, memberships, or publications; Bridal stays
noindex/draft; Family Medicine scope stays conservative and flagged.

**Legend (used in every backlog table):**
- **Intent:** `INFO` (informational) · `COMM` (commercial investigation) · `TXN` (transactional support) · `LOCAL` (local commercial) · `BRAND` (navigational/brand)
- **Funnel:** `TOFU` (awareness) · `MOFU` (consideration) · `BOFU` (decision/support)
- **Type:** `EV` (evergreen) · `TM` (timely)
- **Review:** `C` (clinical review) · `O` (owner/business confirm) · `C+O` (both) · `STD` (standard editorial) · `F` (founder-interview-dependent)
- **Loc:** local-intent strength `H`/`M`/`L`
- **Pri:** `P1` (first / highest) → `P4` (long-tail / fill)
- **Registry mapping:** each row is one future Education article data module. Field order →
  `{ id, title, category, primaryKeyword, intent, funnelStage, relatedService, relatedConcern, localRelevance, evergreen, reviewFlag, priority }`.
  Default `internalLinks = [relatedService?, relatedConcern?, "/assessment", clusterCornerstone]`;
  cornerstone-specific links are defined in **§19 Internal-Linking Blueprint**.

## §2 — Education-category strategy

The 8 approved categories remain appropriate for long-term scale — **keep as-is.** Definitions:

| Category | Editorial purpose | Target reader | Search intent | Belongs | Does NOT belong | Volume | EV:TM |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Aesthetics** | Explain injectable/skin *treatments* at the question level | Considering a treatment | INFO/COMM | timelines, comparisons, myths, candidacy, sequencing | "What is X" duplicating a service page; pricing pages | High (~35% of program) | 95:5 |
| **Skin Health** | Skin/condition education beyond a single procedure | Symptom-first researcher | INFO | causes, home-vs-pro, ingredient science, aging | diagnosis, "treat yourself" protocols | High | 98:2 |
| **Wellness** | Approved wellness services + metabolic/preventive education | Health-motivated adult | INFO/COMM | GLP-1 basics, labs, nutrition, IV honesty | BHRT/hormones/peptides; miracle claims | Medium | 90:10 |
| **Family Medicine** | Primary-care literacy, conservative | Prospective PCP patient | INFO | what primary care is, prevention, when-to-go | unconfirmed scope, insurance specifics | Low–Medium | 97:3 |
| **Patient Guides** | Practical, anxiety-reducing "how to" for AVEN care | Booked or nearly booked | INFO/TXN | prepare/expect/aftercare, choosing a provider, the Assessment | sales copy, hard offers | High (flagship) | 100:0 |
| **AVEN Philosophy** | Brand's clinical worldview in Alaa's voice | Trust-evaluating patient | BRAND | restraint, assessment-first, natural results | generic marketing; anything before founder interview | Low (deliberate) | 100:0 |
| **News & Research** | Contextualized, sourced developments in AVEN's services | Informed patient / press | INFO | FDA/device/GLP-1 research *with clinical context* | generic health-news blogging; hype | Low, rolling | 20:80 |
| **Practice Updates** | Genuine practice milestones | Local community / returning patient | BRAND | openings, new services, tech, events | manufactured "news"; filler | Very low, event-driven | 0:100 |

Relationship to service/concern pages: every category article links **up** to its service or
concern page (commercial/condition intent) and to `/assessment` where a decision is implied —
Education is the top of the funnel that feeds the existing conversion pages, never a replacement.

## §19 — AVEN article-quality standard (minimum bar)

Every AVEN Education article must include, as applicable:
1. **Clear purpose** stated up top; **direct answer near the top** (no burying the lede).
2. **Medically responsible explanation** — mechanism/what-it-is without overpromising.
3. **Who it applies to** and **limitations / when it may not apply.**
4. **When to speak with a provider** (explicit, every clinical topic).
5. **Realistic expectations** — no guaranteed results, no before/after promises.
6. Relevant **AVEN service/concern links** + **Assessment** where a decision is implied.
7. **Reviewer identity** (Alaa Mashal, MSN, APRN, FNP-BC) + **published + last-reviewed dates.**
8. **FAQ** only where genuinely useful (feeds existing FAQ schema pattern).
9. **References** for clinical claims (primary/high-quality sources).
10. **No diagnosis, no filler written for word count, no discount-led framing.**

Favor **quality over volume** — a smaller number of excellent, reviewed articles beats a large
thin library. Reading level: educated-layperson; tone: calm, honest, restrained (AVEN voice).
