Before You Visit

A few good things to know

What happens at a consultation?
A long conversation. We ask about your goals, your history, what's worked, what hasn't. We assess, then we discuss options — but you'll never feel rushed into a decision. Consultations are complimentary and typically run 45–60 minutes.
Do I have to commit to anything?
No. The consultation is a real conversation, not a sales pitch. Many of our patients leave with a plan they think about for weeks before returning.
How should I prepare?
Come without makeup if your visit involves skin assessment. Bring a list of any medications, supplements, and prior treatments. Most importantly, bring your questions.
What about cancellations?
Life happens. We ask for 24 hours' notice when possible. Cancellations and rescheduling can be done directly through the booking link in your confirmation email.
Do you accept insurance?
Aesthetic services are not covered by insurance. Certain wellness services may be — we'll review specifics during your consultation.
What about privacy?
All patient information is held to HIPAA standards. Photographs are only taken with written consent, and never shared without permission.
